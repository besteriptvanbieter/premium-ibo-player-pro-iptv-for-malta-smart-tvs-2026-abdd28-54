How to Set Up Premium IBO Player Pro on Your TV

Setting up the app is simple. Follow these steps:

  1. Buy a subscription from SubTV IP. Choose 1, 3, or 12 months.
  2. After payment, you get your login details by email or WhatsApp.
  3. On your Smart TV, go to the app store and install IBO Player Pro.
  4. Open the app and enter your username and password.
  5. To reduce buffering, set the buffer or cache size to 5,000 ms. The app has a settings menu for this.
  6. To prevent your internet provider from slowing down your stream, use Cloudflare DNS (1.1.1.1). Change the DNS settings in your TV’s network menu.

Once done, you can start watching. The service supports 4K resolution at 60 frames per second with HEVC video codec. HEVC is a video format that gives high quality at lower data use.
